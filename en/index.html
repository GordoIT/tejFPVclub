Who we are

TejFPV is an FPV club from Poznań, Poland. We've been flying together since 2023, mostly racing Tiny Whoops and having a good time.

We're a non-commercial, informal club. we train, organise races, build our own tracks and constantly mess around with drones, electronics and anything else that can make an FPV pilot happy.

We have our own LED race tracks with lap timing, where we meet up, practise and race.

Not everyone is here for racing. Some of us fly freestyle, some just fly for fun after work, and few are into RC planes too.

Basically, if you know what AIO, VTX and ExpressLRS mean, you'll fit right in.

Our teams

Tiny Whoop Team

We fly Tiny Whoops - small FPV drones measuring around 10 cm and weighing just 20-40 grams. Don't let their size fool you. Flying at speed up to 70 km/h through tight race tracks, with no flight stabilization, makes Tiny Whoops suprisingly demanding to fly. They take time to master, but that's also what makes flying them so rewarding. We train all year round on our indoor tracks and regularly compete in the Whoop of Poland league

5-inch Team

The name comes from the classic 5-inch propeller size. these are seriously fast machines - from around 100 g to 1 kg, with speeds of up to 200 km/h. The 5-inch team is most active during spring and summer, when the weather allows us to fly outdoors. During the winter, we switch to simulators, repair our exisitng gear and build new drones.
